Questions, answered

Quick guidance on links, formats, devices, and responsible use.

Which TikTok links can I use?

The interface is intended for direct links to public TikTok videos. Private, restricted, deleted, or region-blocked posts may not be available to a processing service.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout is designed to reflow for phones and tablets, with a large input and touch-friendly controls.

Can I download someone else’s video?

Only save content when you have permission or a lawful reason to do so. Respect creator rights and TikTok’s terms when reusing downloaded material.

Is the downloader connected?

This page is a finished front-end design. A video-processing service must be connected before it can fetch and convert TikTok media.

Use downloads responsibly and respect creator rights.
